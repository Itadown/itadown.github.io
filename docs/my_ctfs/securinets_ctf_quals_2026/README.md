# Securinets CTF Quals 2026

A CTF I did From Saturday 17th of October at 09:00 UTC to Sunday 18th of October at 21:00 UTC in 2026

**Event Details**

* Event Title: Securinets CTF Quals 2026
* Organized By: Securinets is Tunisia's first cybersecurity association, founded in 2003, and this qualifier is its flagship competition: teams play online from anywhere, and the best of them are invited to fight for the title on our campus at INSAT.
* Date: 17th October to 18th October 2026
* Time: 09:00 AM (17th) - 21:00 PM (18th) UTC
* Mode: Online
* Participation: Individual or Team
* Eligibility: Open to everyone. Students, professionals and first-timers all play the same board.
* Discord Server: [https://discord.gg/Xqj6WnNmbQ](https://discord.gg/Xqj6WnNmbQ)

* Website: [https://ctf.securinets.tn/](https://ctf.securinets.tn/)



**Categories**
- Web : SQL injection, auth bypasses, SSRF, SSTI and framework-specific bugs.
- Pwn : Memory corruption from stack to heap, ROP chains and shellcoding.
- Reverse : Static and dynamic analysis of binaries, custom VMs and obfuscated code.
- Crypto : Classical ciphers, RSA, AES, elliptic curves and lattice-based attacks.
- Forensics : Network captures, memory dumps, disk images and steganography.
- OSINT & Misc : Open-source intelligence, geolocation and a mix of miscellaneous challenges.
- Mobile : Android and iOS applications, Frida hooking and mobile-specific bugs.
- Hardware : Firmware analysis, embedded devices, UART and JTAG debugging.



**Rules**
* Solve challenges, submit flags, earn points. Categories are independent.
* Scoring is dynamic: challenges lose value as more teams solve them.
* No attacking the competition platform or its infrastructure.
* No flag sharing, brute forcing the flag endpoint, or collaborating between teams.
* Automated scanners against challenge hosts are not allowed.
* Violations lead to disqualification. Organizer decisions are final.
